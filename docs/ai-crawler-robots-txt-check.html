Is your robots.txt blocking ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude?

One line in robots.txt can remove a site from AI answers. It often happens by accident: a security plugin, a CDN setting or a copied template adds a block for "AI bots", and nobody notices until traffic from AI assistants drops.

This guide explains which AI crawlers matter, how to check your site in two minutes, and how to get an alert if it changes later.

Two kinds of AI crawlers

AI companies use different crawlers for different jobs. The difference matters:

  • Search and answer crawlers fetch pages so the assistant can show and cite them in answers. Block these and your site can stop appearing in that assistant's answers.
  • Training crawlers collect pages to train future models. Blocking these is a policy choice and does not remove you from search answers by itself.

OpenAI (ChatGPT)OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-UserGPTBot
Anthropic (Claude)Claude-SearchBot, Claude-UserClaudeBot
PerplexityPerplexityBot, Perplexity-User—
Google (Gemini)Regular GooglebotGoogle-Extended (a control token, not a separate crawler)

Names come from each company's crawler documentation and can change. Check the official pages before you edit robots.txt.

Check your site in two minutes

  1. Open https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt in a browser.
  2. Look for a User-agent: line with one of the names above, followed by Disallow: /.
  3. Also look at User-agent: *. If it has Disallow: /, every crawler is blocked, including Google.

This blocks ChatGPT search:

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Disallow: /

This blocks training only and keeps you in ChatGPT search answers:

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /
Some CDNs and hosting panels have a "block AI bots" switch that answers at the server level, not in robots.txt. If your robots.txt looks fine but AI assistants still cannot read your pages, check that setting too.

Other things that hide a page from AI answers

  • A noindex meta tag or X-Robots-Tag header, often added by a plugin update or a staging setting that went live
  • A missing or broken XML sitemap
  • Pages with almost no readable text (content only inside images or scripts)

Summary

  • Search crawlers decide whether you appear in AI answers. Training crawlers do not.
  • Check /robots.txt for the names in the table above, and for User-agent: * with Disallow: /.
  • Settings change over time, so a scheduled check catches problems before traffic drops.
